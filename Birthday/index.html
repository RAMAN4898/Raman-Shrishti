A Letter From The Heart

Meri Shrishti,

There are moments in life that shift everything — quietly, completely, without warning. Meeting you was one of them. You walked in and gently rearranged the furniture of my heart, and somehow every single piece fell into exactly the right place. Tum aayi toh laga jaise zindagi ko uski manzil mil gayi.

Happy Birthday, my love. I am the luckiest person alive, simply because you chose me too.

— Always, and completely, yours,
Raman ♥
